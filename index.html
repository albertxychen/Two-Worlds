A fogbound courtyard, a long stone bridge, and a gate with two mouths. Walk with the dead. Look closely. Listen harder. File them where they belong.

  4. At the mark, press 1 for Heaven or 2 for Hell.
  5. Correct filings pay Soul Coins. Press Tab to spend them.
